Treaty 7 Impact

How Treaty 7 shaped land, rights, and communities.

Effects of Treaty 7

Treaty 7 had lasting effects on the First Nations who signed it and on the development of western Canada. The agreement reshaped how land was used and how Indigenous peoples lived across southern Alberta.

For many First Nations, the treaty meant a major shift in daily life. Communities that had moved across the plains now lived on designated reserve lands. Traditional ways of hunting, gathering, and traveling were affected.

Impact on Land

  • Large areas of southern Alberta were opened to settlers and government use.

  • Reserve lands were set aside for each of the five signing nations.

  • First Nations retained hunting and fishing rights in the treaty area.

  • The size and location of reserves were decided by the government, not always reflecting what First Nations expected.

Impact on Communities

Life on reserves brought significant changes. Access to traditional food sources became harder as buffalo numbers fell. Many families depended on government rations and farming programs introduced through the treaty.

Schools were established on or near reserves. These were often residential schools where children were separated from their families and cultures, leaving deep harm that continues to affect communities today.

Impact on Rights

  • Treaty rights are protected under Canada's Constitution Act of 1982.

  • First Nations have used the treaty in court cases to defend land and resource rights.

  • Ongoing disagreements exist about what the treaty promised versus what was delivered.

  • The treaty remains a living agreement that affects government decisions today.

Legacy Today

The five nations of Treaty 7 continue to advocate for their rights and work within the treaty framework. Many leaders and communities are involved in negotiations with federal and provincial governments over land use, resource development, and self-governance.

Reconciliation efforts in Canada have brought greater attention to the promises made in treaties like Treaty 7 and whether they have been honoured.

Key Impacts

  • Reserve lands were created for all five Treaty 7 nations.

  • Treaty rights are still legally recognized in Canada today.

  • Residential schools caused lasting cultural and family harm.

  • First Nations continue to negotiate treaty rights with the government.

  • The treaty influences land and resource decisions in Alberta.
